Model a charging site where a battery buffers a limited grid connection — for any charger. Pick a market and an EV (real charge curves from EVKX.net), set traffic, and watch the buffer drain, the grid refill it, and the chargers throttle when it empties. The Lab tab adds traffic shaping, solar, efficiency, and demand-charge economics.

How to read it teaching model

    Adaptive timestep (5–60 s). Supply order solar→grid→battery; each charger cabinet shares its power across its ports; the battery fills the gap to per-cable & site caps and recharges from surplus at the charge C-rate × round-trip efficiency; throttle = proportional curtailment when demand > available supply. Poisson arrivals (seeded, reproducible) occupy free ports; a busy site queues. Real DC charge curves from EVKX.net (SoC→kW); the Denza Z9 GT curve is calibrated to a measured real-world flash session (10→97% in ~9 min); the heavy-duty-truck curve is a synthetic illustration. A teaching model, not a vendor controller emulation.
